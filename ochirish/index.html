Ma‘lumotni o‘chirish

Dengiz’dagi ma‘lumotlaringizni istalgan vaqt o‘chirtirishingiz mumkin. Buning uchun:

  1. sellersunnatuzum@gmail.com manziliga «Ma‘lumotni o‘chirish» mavzusi bilan xat yozing.
  2. Xatda ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan email va telefon raqamingizni yozing, shunda hisobingizni aniqlaymiz.
  3. So‘rovni qabul qilganimizni 3 ish kuni ichida tasdiqlaymiz va ma‘lumotlaringizni 30 kun ichida o‘chiramiz.

Nima o‘chiriladi

  • Hisob ma‘lumoti (ism, email, telefon, Telegram, biznes).
  • Meta reklama akkauntidan yuklangan statistika va o‘zgarishlar jurnali.
  • CRM’dan kelgan sotuv bosqichi yozuvlari va ulanish tokenlari.
  • Telegram bildirishnoma ulanishi va saqlangan CAPI kalitlari.

Meta ruxsatini o‘zingiz bekor qilish

Business Manager → Business Settings → Hamkorlar (Partners) bo‘limida Dengiz’ni olib tashlang. Shundan so‘ng Meta ma‘lumotini yangilay olmaymiz.

Batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your data from Dengiz at any time:

  1. Email sellersunnatuzum@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the email and phone number you registered with so we can identify your account.
  3. We confirm the request within 3 business days and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Account data (name, email, phone, Telegram, business).
  • Statistics and change log imported from your Meta ad account.
  • Sales-stage records received from your CRM and connection tokens.
  • The Telegram notification link and stored Conversions API keys.

Revoking Meta access yourself

In Business Manager → Business Settings → Partners, remove Dengiz. After that we can no longer refresh your Meta data.
