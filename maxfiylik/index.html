Dengiz — reklama natijasini kuzatib, o‘zgarishlar haqida xabar beradigan va sifatli lid signalini Meta’ga qaytaradigan xizmat. Quyida qanday ma‘lumot bilan ishlashimiz yozilgan.

1. Qanday ma‘lumot yig‘amiz

  • Hisob ma‘lumoti: ism, email, telefon raqam, Telegram username (ixtiyoriy), biznes nomi.
  • Meta reklama ma‘lumoti: siz bergan faqat ko‘rish huquqi orqali reklama akkauntining tuzilmasi (kampaniya, guruh, reklama nomlari va holati), xarajat, ko‘rsatuv, bosish, lid soni, yosh/jins/hudud/platforma bo‘yicha jamlangan ko‘rsatkichlar, video ko‘rish ko‘rsatkichlari va o‘zgarishlar jurnali.
  • Sotuv bosqichlari: CRM yoki boshqa tizimingiz yuborgan hodisalar (lid, sifatli lid, uchrashuv, sotuv), summa va mijozning telefon raqami.
  • Telegram: bot orqali bildirishnoma olish uchun chat identifikatori.

Biz Meta akkauntingiz parolini so‘ramaymiz va reklamalaringizni o‘zgartirmaymiz.

2. Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Dashboard va hisobotlarni ko‘rsatish, AI tahlil va tavsiyalar berish.
  • Reklamadagi o‘zgarishlar va muammolar haqida sizga xabar yuborish (saytda va Telegram’da).
  • Siz roziligingizni bergan holda sifatli lid signalini Meta Conversions API orqali reklama akkauntingizga qaytarish.
  • Siz bilan bog‘lanish va xizmatni yaxshilash.

Ma‘lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama maqsadida uchinchi tomonlarga bermaymiz. Meta’dan olingan ma‘lumot faqat sizga xizmat ko‘rsatish uchun ishlatiladi.

3. Mijoz telefon raqamlari

Mijozlarning telefon raqamlari bazada qaytarib bo‘lmaydigan shifrlangan (HMAC) ko‘rinishda saqlanadi. Meta’ga signal yuborish uchun SHA-256 hash tayyorlanadi, u yuborilgach o‘chiriladi. Meta’ga faqat hash yuboriladi, ochiq raqam emas.

4. Qayerda va qancha saqlaymiz

Ma‘lumotlar Supabase (AWS, Yevropa, Frankfurt) ma‘lumotlar bazasida saqlanadi, kirish rollar bo‘yicha cheklangan, maxfiy kalitlar alohida omborda. Hisob faol bo‘lguncha va undan keyin ko‘pi bilan 30 kun saqlanadi, so‘ngra o‘chiriladi. Qonun talab qilgan hisob-kitob yozuvlari bundan mustasno.

5. Huquqlaringiz

Ma‘lumotlaringizni ko‘rish, tuzatish, ko‘chirib olish yoki o‘chirishni so‘rashingiz, Meta ruxsatini istalgan vaqt bekor qilishingiz mumkin (Meta Business Settings → Hamkorlar). O‘chirish tartibi: ma‘lumotni o‘chirish.

6. Uchinchi tomonlar

Xizmat ko‘rsatish uchun quyidagilardan foydalanamiz: Supabase (baza), Vercel (sayt), Meta (reklama ma‘lumoti), Telegram (bildirishnoma) va, agar yoqilgan bo‘lsa, Anthropic (AI xulosa matni, faqat jamlangan raqamlar bilan, shaxsiy ma‘lumotsiz).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Dengiz is a service that monitors advertising results, alerts you to changes, and sends quality-lead signals back to Meta. This policy explains what data we handle.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, Telegram username (optional), business name.
  • Meta advertising data: through the view-only access you grant, the structure of your ad account (campaign, ad set and ad names and statuses), spend, impressions, clicks, leads, aggregated age/gender/region/platform breakdowns, video metrics, and the account change log.
  • Sales stages: events sent by your CRM or other systems (lead, qualified, meeting, purchase), amounts, and customer phone numbers.
  • Telegram: a chat identifier, to deliver notifications through the bot.

We never ask for your Meta password and we do not change your ads.

2. How we use it

  • To show dashboards and reports and to provide AI analysis and recommendations.
  • To notify you about changes and problems in your advertising (on the site and in Telegram).
  • With your consent, to send quality-lead signals to your ad account through the Meta Conversions API.
  • To contact you and improve the service.

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. Data received from Meta is used only to provide the service to you.

3. Customer phone numbers

Customer phone numbers are stored only in an irreversible keyed hash (HMAC). To send a signal to Meta, a SHA-256 hash is prepared and deleted after it is sent. Only the hash, never the plain number, is sent to Meta.

4. Where and how long

Data is stored in a Supabase database (AWS, Frankfurt, EU) with role-based access; secrets are kept in a separate vault. Data is kept while your account is active and for up to 30 days after, then deleted, except billing records required by law.

5. Your rights

You can request access, correction, export or deletion of your data, and you can revoke Meta access at any time (Meta Business Settings → Partners). See data deletion instructions.

6. Third parties

We use Supabase (database), Vercel (website), Meta (advertising data), Telegram (notifications) and, if enabled, Anthropic (AI summary text, using aggregated numbers only, without personal data).
